Content types proposed for a CMS: service, programme, person, partner, insight episode, research item, article/op-ed, media release, book, and social enterprise initiative. Tags and cross-links should connect each insight to relevant services, programmes, authors and initiatives. Initial content volume does not justify on-site search or complex filtering; add these only after the approved archive volume is known.

## Minimum visitor journeys represented

1. Executive: Home -> Crisis or Strategic Communications -> related C-suite programme -> enquiry.
2. HR / Learning & Development: Home -> C-suite programmes -> programme topic -> programme enquiry.
3. Sponsor / community partner: Home -> Social Enterprise -> Money Wise Tales or Entrepreneurship Reality Check -> partnership enquiry.
4. Reader: Home -> CF Insights, Research, article, release or book -> external source or relevant contact route.

The mobile navigation uses the same section links. The enquiry preview asks for name, work email, organisation, topic and message; it includes a consent checkbox, native validation and an inaccessible-to-humans honeypot field. The prototype opens an email draft and does not implement a real spam service or secure server-side handling.

## Proposed production checks (not performed)

- Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA. Verify keyboard operation, screen-reader labels, contrast, focus states, responsive layout, image alternatives, form errors and confirmation behaviour.
- Performance: optimize and size images, avoid unnecessary embeds, and set measurable desktop/mobile acceptance targets during discovery.
- Measurement: baseline existing analytics first; then agree events for successful enquiries, programme interest, social enterprise enquiries and engagement with insights. No analytics script is included in this concept.
- Content approval: Citrine must approve names, titles, categories, partner status, article metadata, book purchase routes, photo rights, logos and final copy.
- Privacy and data: Citrine must provide an approved notice and choose form delivery, access controls, retention, hosting location and deletion process.
- Platform: CMS, hosting, staging/production, backups, security, SEO migration and support remain to be selected with Citrine. The current files are a static prototype only.
- Language: English is the concept language. Additional languages would need approved translations and separate scope.
